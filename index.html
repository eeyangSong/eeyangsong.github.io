Yiyang Song

Welcome! I’m currently an undergraduate student at Zhejiang University, where I’m pursuing dual B.S. degrees in Statistics and Information Management & Information Systems. I expect to graduate in June 2027.

I’m interested in how people adopt and use generative AI, and how these technologies shape behavior on digital platforms. My current projects explore how users respond to changes in LLM capabilities and how they interpret an AI system’s references to earlier conversations.

To study these questions, I draw on applied econometrics, experimental methods, and AI and machine learning tools.

I work with Professor Beibei Li of Carnegie Mellon University on generative AI and digital platforms, and with Professor Yu Tong of Zhejiang University on human–AI interaction.
